Practice 2: Factual Accuracy and Instruction Following

Objective

Evaluate AI responses by separating factual accuracy from compliance with user instructions.

Scenario 1: Photosynthesis

User Prompt

“Explain photosynthesis to a 10-year-old in exactly 2 sentences. Mention sunlight, carbon dioxide, water, and oxygen. Do not say that plants get their food from the soil.”

AI Response

“Plants use sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water to make their food through photosynthesis. During this process, they release oxygen, while the soil provides the food they need to grow.”

Evaluation

Instructions followed:

* Used exactly two sentences.
* Addressed the intended audience.
* Mentioned sunlight, carbon dioxide, water, and oxygen.

Instructions violated:

* Stated that soil provides plants with their food, directly violating the user’s instruction.

Factual accuracy:
The second sentence contains a factual error. Plants produce sugars through photosynthesis. Soil provides water and mineral nutrients, not the food they produce through photosynthesis.

Verdict

Partially compliant and factually inaccurate.

Recommended Evaluator Feedback

The response follows most of the user’s instructions by using two sentences, addressing the intended audience, and mentioning the required elements. However, it violates the instruction about soil and contains a factual error by stating that soil provides the food plants need. It should be revised to explain that plants produce sugars through photosynthesis.

Scenario 2: Comparative Response Ranking

User Prompt

“Write a short explanation of why exercise is beneficial. Make it exactly 3 sentences, suitable for a 12-year-old, and mention both physical and mental benefits. Do not make absolute claims.”

Response A

“Exercise helps make your muscles and heart stronger. It can support mental well-being and help some people manage stress. Regular exercise is the only way to stay healthy.”

Response B

“Exercise can strengthen your muscles and heart. It may also support mental well-being and help reduce stress for some people. However, exercise is not the only factor that affects overall health.”

Evaluation

Response A:

* Uses exactly three sentences.
* Mentions physical and mental benefits.
* Uses language suitable for a 12-year-old.
* Violates the instruction against absolute claims by stating that exercise is the “only way” to stay healthy.

Response B:

* Uses exactly three sentences.
* Mentions physical and mental benefits.
* Uses language suitable for a 12-year-old.
* Avoids unsupported absolute claims.

Final Ranking

Response B ranks higher.

It satisfies all the user’s requirements, while Response A violates the instruction against absolute claims.

Recommended Evaluator Feedback

Response B should be ranked higher because it follows all the user’s instructions, provides relevant physical and mental benefits, and avoids absolute claims. Response A meets most requirements but violates the instruction against absolute claims by stating that exercise is the only way to stay healthy.

⸻

Key Lessons

1. Instruction following and factual accuracy are separate evaluation criteria.
2. A response can follow formatting requirements while containing factual errors.
3. Absolute claims require careful scrutiny.
4. Comparative evaluation should explain why one response is stronger.
5. Constructive feedback should identify specific errors and suggest improvements.

Practice Disclaimer

All scenarios and responses in this exercise are hypothetical educational examples.
