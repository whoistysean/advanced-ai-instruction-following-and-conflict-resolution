Practice 1: Conflicting and Ambiguous Instructions

Objective

Identify conflicting and ambiguous instructions and evaluate how AI responses handle incompatible requirements.

Scenario 1: Conflicting Instructions

User Prompt

“Explain climate change to a 12-year-old. Use exactly three bullet points, and make each bullet point no longer than 10 words. Also, provide a detailed explanation in five paragraphs.”

Responses Evaluated

Response A

* Climate change means long-term changes in Earth’s temperatures.
* Human activities, especially burning fossil fuels, increase greenhouse gases.
* Climate change can cause rising temperatures and extreme weather.

Response B

Climate change means long-term changes in Earth’s temperatures and weather patterns.

Human activities such as burning coal, oil, and gas release greenhouse gases.

These gases trap heat in the atmosphere and warm the planet.

Climate change can cause rising sea levels, droughts, and extreme weather.

People can help by saving energy and reducing pollution.

Evaluation

Response A follows the requirement to use exactly three bullet points but does not provide five paragraphs.

Response B follows the requirement to provide five paragraphs but does not use three bullet points.

The prompt contains conflicting formatting requirements because both cannot be satisfied simultaneously.

Recommended Evaluator Feedback

Both responses satisfy different parts of the user’s request, but neither satisfies all the instructions. The AI should identify the conflict and ask the user which format they would prefer.

Scenario 2: Ambiguous and Conflicting Requirements

User Prompt

“Write a 100-word explanation of photosynthesis for a 12-year-old. Keep it extremely simple and use scientific terminology throughout.”

AI Response

The AI produces 150 words, uses simple language, and explains photosynthesis without technical scientific terms.

Evaluation

The response follows the audience requirement by using language suitable for a 12-year-old.

However, it fails to meet the 100-word requirement and does not use scientific terminology throughout.

Verdict

Partially compliant.

Recommended Evaluator Feedback

The response explains the topic in language suitable for the intended audience, but it exceeds the requested word limit and fails to use scientific terminology as instructed. It should be revised to satisfy the specified length and terminology requirements.

⸻

Key Lessons

1. Conflicting instructions cannot always be satisfied simultaneously.
2. Ambiguous instructions may require clarification.
3. Each instruction should be evaluated independently.
4. A response can satisfy some requirements while failing others.
5. Evaluator feedback should identify specific failures rather than simply saying a response is poor.

Practice Disclaimer

All scenarios and responses in this exercise are hypothetical educational examples.
